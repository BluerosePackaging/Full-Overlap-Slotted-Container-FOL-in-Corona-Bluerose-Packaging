## How the Full-Overlap Design Works

Unlike a regular slotted container, where the outer flaps meet near the center, an FOL box uses longer flaps that extend across most or all of the opening. Once folded and secured, these flaps create overlapping layers on the top and bottom panels.

This construction can be particularly helpful when cartons are exposed to rough handling or stacked on their sides. The additional board layers provide more protection at the ends, although the actual load capacity still depends on the corrugated material, box dimensions, closure method, and shipping conditions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does FOL mean in corrugated packaging?

FOL stands for Full Overlap Slotted Container. It is a corrugated box style with extended flaps that overlap across the top and bottom when closed.

### How is an FOL box different from a regular slotted container?

A regular slotted container generally has outer flaps that meet at the center. An FOL carton has longer outer flaps that overlap, providing additional corrugated layers across the closure panels.

### Are full-overlap cartons suitable for heavy products?

They can be suitable for many heavy products, depending on the carton dimensions, corrugated board strength, product weight, and handling conditions. The design alone does not guarantee a particular load capacity.

### Can FOL cartons be used for fragile items?

Yes, when the carton is appropriately selected and combined with suitable internal cushioning. Foam, dividers, or protective pads may help prevent movement and reduce damage from impacts.

### What information should I provide when requesting a quote?

### Can these cartons be stored before assembly?
